title: Poverty drops, health insurance coverage rises in Maine, but slower than nation
date: 2017-09-14
---

New Census Bureau data shows Maine made progress on key economic indicators in 2016, with poverty declining and private health insurance coverage rising, though the state lagged behind national improvements. The release includes comprehensive data on economic, social, and housing characteristics at state, county, and local levels, with Portland being the only Maine city included in one-year survey estimates.
