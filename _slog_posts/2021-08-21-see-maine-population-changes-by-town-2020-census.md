title: See how local Maine populations changed in the 2020 census
date: 2021-08-21
---

Redistricting data from the 2020 Census shows Maine's population grew 2.6 percent to 1,340,320, with southern Maine communities experiencing the most growth during the decade. An interactive map visualizes population changes by municipality, revealing both gains in southern Maine and some growth in small rural communities, providing a detailed view of demographic shifts across the state.
