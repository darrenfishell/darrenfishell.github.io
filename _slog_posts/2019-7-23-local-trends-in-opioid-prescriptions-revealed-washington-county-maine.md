title: See the towns where opioid prescriptions soared in Maine from 2006 to 2012
date: 2019-7-23
---

Analysis of DEA prescription data acquired through a Washington Post FOIA lawsuit reveals Penobscot County led Maine in opioid pills per person from 2006-2012, with three Washington County communities (Marshfield, Calais, Machias) receiving outsized prescription totals. The data shows a "virtual road map to the nation's opioid epidemic," with Bangor topping all communities in average annual pills distributed, though smaller communities like Newport, Lincoln, and Patten had higher per-capita rates in Penobscot County.
