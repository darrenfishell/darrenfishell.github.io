title: Consultants and others chime in on Maine marijuana rulemaking
date: 2017-09-13
---

After public pressure, Maine state government published recommendations from various stakeholders regarding marijuana legalization rulemaking, including responses from the Maine CDC, cannabis tech firm Weedmaps, and others. These documents provide insight into early regulatory discussions and reveal how businesses are positioning themselves to enter the legal cannabis market, with real estate acquisitions already underway.
