title: Maine GIS & DEP offer detailed look at CMP's proposed power lines
date: 2019-02-09
---

The Maine Department of Environmental Protection published detailed maps of Central Maine Power's controversial New England Clean Energy Connect project, showing exact routes for two power lines and intersections with critical habitats for Atlantic salmon, lynx, and rare plants. These maps provide much higher detail than the project's public website, available as a downloadable KMZ file with multiple environmental data layers.
