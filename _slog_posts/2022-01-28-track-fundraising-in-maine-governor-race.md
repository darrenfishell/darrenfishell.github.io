title: Track contributions to Maine's gubernatorial candidates
date: 2022-01-28

Interactive dashboards tracking campaign contributions in Maine's 2022 gubernatorial race, dominated by Democratic incumbent Janet Mills and returning former Gov. Paul LePage. The visualizations show contribution summaries and detailed contributor lists, with several donors already maxing out at $3,450 to both candidates, including former Google CEO Eric Schmidt for Mills and liquor magnate Paul Coulombe for LePage.
