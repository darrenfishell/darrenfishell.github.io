title: How will Maine pick areas to receive new business tax benefits?
date: 2018-02-27
---

Maine officials must select 31 of 123 low-income census tracts for the new Opportunity Zone program, part of the 2017 federal tax overhaul that incentivizes investments through capital gains tax benefits. The state's Department of Economic and Community Development is soliciting public comments on selection criteria, with an interactive map allowing exploration of different variables that might influence the decision-making process.
