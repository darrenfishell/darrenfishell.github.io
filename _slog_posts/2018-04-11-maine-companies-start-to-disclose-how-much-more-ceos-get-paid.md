layout: post
title: Maine CEO makes 121 times the mid-range employee, according to new disclosures
date: 2018-04-11
---

Under new Dodd-Frank disclosure rules, Idexx CEO Jonathan Ayers makes 121 times the median employee salary of $55,202, marking the first major Maine public company to report CEO-to-employee pay ratios. While this ratio is significant, it pales in comparison to national extremes like Mattel's 4,987:1 ratio, with Maine banks generally showing lower ratios (ranging from 14.7:1 to 47:1).
