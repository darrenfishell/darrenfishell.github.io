title: A reference for selected Opportunity Zones in Maine
date: 2019-5-30
---

An updated interactive map of Maine's 31 designated Opportunity Zones, census tracts where investors can receive federal tax benefits, using Tableau 2019.2's high-resolution vector maps to show exact census tract boundaries down to street level. The map helps visualize these often-unfamiliar geographies, with one example showing how Bangor's designated zone excludes downtown and mall areas that city officials consider crucial for development.
