title: Residential electricity ripoff accelerates for the first time in five years
date: 2021-10-21
---

Electricity retailers charged Maine households $175 million more than standard offer rates from 2012-2020, with 2020 marking the first year since 2015 that the premium increased, totaling $20.5 million last year alone. Despite declining enrollment, retail suppliers' rates dropped slower than standard offer rates, with Electricity Maine dominating the market while its customer base shrank from 136,796 in 2013 to 36,085 in 2020, having consistently charged above-market rates since its first year.
