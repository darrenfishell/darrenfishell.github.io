title: Company getting $1M from Maine pays median global employee $11,035
date: 2018-04-14
---

ON Semiconductor, which received a $1 million grant from Maine for a $23 million plant upgrade, disclosed its CEO makes 972 times the median global employee salary of $11,035. The company attributes the low median to cost-of-living differences across its international operations, with only 4,724 of its 33,500 employees based in North America.
