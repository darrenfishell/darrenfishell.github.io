title: Susan Collins dominated fundraising, but not in Maine
date: 2019-6-16
---

Incumbent Sen. Susan Collins received only 20 percent of her individual contributions from Maine donors, while Democratic challenger Sara Gideon raised nearly three times Collins' Maine haul in the latest quarter. Interactive visualizations break down state-by-state donations and allow searching individual Maine donors, using Tableau's new set actions feature to enable interactive filtering.
