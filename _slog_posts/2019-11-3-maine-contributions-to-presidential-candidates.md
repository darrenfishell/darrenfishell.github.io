title: See the 2020 candidates hauling in the most cash from Maine donors
date: 2019-11-3
---

Bernie Sanders led all 2020 presidential candidates in Maine fundraising through September 2019, pulling in more than $131,000 from 504 contributors, the only Democrat to outraise incumbent Donald Trump in the state. Interactive visualizations map individual donations by location and show fundraising timelines, with Elizabeth Warren and Pete Buttigieg ranking next highest among Democratic candidates.
