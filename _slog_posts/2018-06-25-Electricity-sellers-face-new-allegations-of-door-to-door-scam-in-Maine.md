title: One customer says new, misleading electricity sales pitch is happening statewide
date: 2018-06-25
---

New allegations in a potential class-action lawsuit claim door-to-door salespeople for Electricity Maine posed as Central Maine Power "auditors" to trick customers into contracts. Salespeople allegedly told customers they were sent to correct inappropriately high bills, with reports emerging from multiple service territories, suggesting the deceptive practice extends beyond CMP's coverage area.
