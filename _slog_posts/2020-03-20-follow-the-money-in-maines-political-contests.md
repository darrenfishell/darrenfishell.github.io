title: Follow the money in Maine's 2020 political contests
date: 2020-03-20
---

Maine's competitive Senate race attracted more than $20 million in contributions and outside spending months before Election Day, with interactive dashboards aggregating direct contributions, outside group spending, and party expenditures. The dashboards update nightly from FEC data, combining disparate campaign finance sources to show total influence in each race, with support amounts added to candidate totals and opposition spending shown as negative values.
