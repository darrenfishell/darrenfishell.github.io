title: Track Maine coronavirus cases and rates by county
date: 2021-07-13
---

Interactive dashboards tracking COVID-19 cases in Maine by county, updated daily from Maine CDC data scraped hourly and stored in a time series. The visualizations include logarithmic scale charts to show growth trends, county-level curves starting from each area's first day with 10+ cases, and doubling time calculations for each state, providing tools to assess whether areas are avoiding exponential spread.
