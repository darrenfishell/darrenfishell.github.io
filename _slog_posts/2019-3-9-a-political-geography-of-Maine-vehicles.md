title: A political geography of Maine, or how winning truck town wasn't enough for Shawn Moody
date: 2019-3-9
---

Analysis of 2018 gubernatorial election results and vehicle registration data reveals that Republican Shawn Moody won three-quarters of Maine's "truck towns" (where Ford F-150, Chevy Silverado, or GMC Sierra are most popular), but his margins there were relatively slim compared to Democrat Janet Mills' dominance in Subaru communities. While Moody won most truck towns, Mills' nearly two-to-one margin in non-truck areas and her 25:1 lead in Subaru Forester communities proved decisive, highlighting the urban-rural political divide reflected in vehicle preferences.
