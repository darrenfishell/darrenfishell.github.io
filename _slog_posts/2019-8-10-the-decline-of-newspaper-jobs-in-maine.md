title: The decline of newspaper jobs in Maine, in six sad charts
date: 2019-8-10
---

Maine lost more than half its newspaper publishing jobs from 2000 to 2018, dropping from 2,526 to just over 1,100, with wage growth also lagging other industries. The decline stands to increase government waste and decrease voter turnout, with the industry showing some of the steepest job losses and slowest wage growth among all Maine industries, unlike manufacturing where automation maintained output despite job losses.
