title: An old, struggling industry gets another new glossy brochure
date: 2017-10-05
---

Stored Solar LLC, a subsidized biomass generator with outstanding debts and back taxes, continues to produce promotional materials for ambitious projects despite financial struggles. The company's affiliate "Born Global" posted a new vision for biomass plants online, including questionable testimonials and overstated partnerships, while seeking additional taxpayer support through loans and grants from Maine economic development authorities.
