title: Droning misty fall foliage over Maine's Dead River
date: 2017-10-11
---

Aerial footage from a camping trip near Maine's Dead River during an annual Ural motorcycle rally, showcasing misty fall foliage captured from 400 feet up. The video represents the first real mission of 2017 for the author's drone, the "Airship Sancho," and marks the beginning of a regular feature on drone footage alongside data reporting work.
