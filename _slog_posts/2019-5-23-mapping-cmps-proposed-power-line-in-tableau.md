title: Mapping CMP's controversial power line proposal in Tableau 2019.2
date: 2019-5-23
---

An interactive map showing Central Maine Power's New England Clean Energy Connect project route through 37 communities, with 15 communities opposing or rescinding support. The visualization leverages Tableau 2019.2's improved spatial file handling and vector maps, using spatial joins to combine project files with municipal boundaries and community support/opposition data, demonstrating new geospatial capabilities in the software.
