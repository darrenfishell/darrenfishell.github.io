title: Maine hobby drone registrations surge 160 percent in 2017
date: 2018-04-09
---

Hobbyist drone registrations in Maine jumped to 7,416 by January 2018, a nearly 160 percent increase from February 2017, according to FAA data. The Portland area added the most drones, followed by Bangor and other southern Maine communities, with registrations more than doubling in the state's most populated areas and nearly tripling in Lewiston.
