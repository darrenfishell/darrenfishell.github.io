title: Maine households could have saved $200 million on electricity in past decade
date: 2025-10-23
---

Maine households paying above-market rates for electricity jumped sharply in 2024 after hitting a nine-year low in 2023, spending $67.6 million with retail electricity providers—roughly $20 million more than the standard offer rate. Since 2012, Mainers would have saved over $200 million by using the default standard offer, with the 2023 Office of the Public Advocate study recommending closing the residential market entirely, as higher costs disproportionately affect low-income households in southern and central Maine.
