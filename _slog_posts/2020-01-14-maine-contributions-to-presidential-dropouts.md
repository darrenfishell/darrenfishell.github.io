title: Maine donors have so far given $81,000 to presidential dropouts
date: 2020-01-14
---

Maine political donors contributed at least $81,000 to presidential candidates who later dropped out of the 2020 race, with Kamala Harris leading at $24,344 through September 2019. An interactive map visualizes all individual donations to both active and withdrawn candidates, showing the geographic distribution of contributions across the state.
