title: WEX posts highest CEO-employee pay disparity in Maine, but rejects comparison
date: 2018-04-25
---

WEX disclosed CEO Melissa Smith earns 173 times the median employee salary of $62,621, the highest ratio among Maine companies but still far below national extremes. Despite the disclosure requirement, WEX explicitly rejected using these figures for comparative purposes, arguing they don't provide meaningful evaluation of management effectiveness or business prospects.
