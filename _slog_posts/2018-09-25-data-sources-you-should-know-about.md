A practical guide to federal data sources for local reporters, focusing on the American Community Survey's comparative tables and the Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages. The post explains how to navigate these datasets to find statistically significant changes in communities and compare local economies to national trends, with additional resources for discovering other valuable data sources.
