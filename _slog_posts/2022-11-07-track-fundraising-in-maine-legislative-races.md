title: Cash to Maine legislative candidates jumps 50% from 2020
date: 2022-11-07
---

Maine's 2022 legislative contests saw a 50 percent increase in campaign contributions and spending compared to 2020, topping $12 million for House and Senate candidates. The surge was fueled by competitive races, with the northernmost Senate district race between Senate President Troy Jackson and Rep. Sue Bernard attracting over $1.1 million, both using Maine's public campaign financing system.
