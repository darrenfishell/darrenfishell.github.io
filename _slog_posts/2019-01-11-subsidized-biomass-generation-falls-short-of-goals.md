title: Biomass generator missed at least 80 percent of subsidies
date: 2019-01-11
---

Stored Solar, a subsidized biomass generator, captured less than 20 percent of the $9.4 million in taxpayer subsidies it could have received under a two-year legislative bailout, generating only enough power to qualify for $2.1 million by the end of 2018. The company dramatically cut employment from a peak of 77 workers in December 2017 to just 14 by May 2018, while another bailout recipient, ReEnergy, captured about 75 percent of its possible subsidies before shuttering one of two plants.
