I'm a data scientist relocating to Los Angeles, California, and seeking new professional opportunities. I have worked in business intelligence roles for the past 7 years, after a nine-year career in journalism, where I worked as an investigative, business and general assignment reporter at publications in Maine and California.

I grew up in the suburb of [Diamond Bar, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Bar,_California), before coming to Maine [for college](http://bowdoin.edu). Maine is amazing and I stayed in Portland, where my wife and I met and have lived since 2011. 

I started my journalism career as the first local editor for [Patch.com](https://patch.com/users/darren-fishell) in my hometown. I came back to Maine for a staff job at a small, [afternoon daily paper](http://timesrecord.com) in Brunswick. I developed a fondness for data reporting at [Mainebiz](http://mainebiz.com) before three years covering Maine's economy and investigations at the [Bangor Daily News](http://bangordailynews.com).

Witnessing some live data science and statistics wizardry one election night at the Bangor Daily News (when the paper had a data scientist on staff!) fascinated me and pushed me in a new direction.

I most recently managed business intelligence tools for the accounting firm [Baker Newman Noyes](https://www.bnncpa.com/), until April 2025. I left the position to move to Los Angeles to be closer to family (and the Hollywood Bowl). I also received a Master's degree in data science in fall 2024 from Northeastern University, through its new Portland, Maine campus.
