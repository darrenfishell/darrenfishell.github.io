I'm a data scientist and business intelligence specialist based in Los Angeles, California. I work on BI tools at [Relativity Space](https://www.relativityspace.com/). I have worked in business intelligence roles since 2018, after a nine-year career in journalism, where I worked as an investigative, business, and general assignment reporter at publications in Maine and California.

I grew up in the suburb of [Diamond Bar, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_Bar,_California), before going to Maine [for college](http://bowdoin.edu). Maine is amazing and I stayed in Portland, where my wife and I met and lived from 2011 to 2025, when we relocated to Los Angeles to be closer to family (and LA is also lovely).

Before Relativity, I started the business intelligence practice at the regional accounting firm [Baker Newman Noyes](https://www.bnncpa.com/). Before that, I worked primarily in Tableau as a BI consultant at Arkatechture, working for clients including Cole Haan, Sony and L.L.Bean.

Before _that_, I started my journalism career as the first local editor for [Patch.com](https://patch.com/users/darren-fishell) in my hometown. I came back to Maine for a staff job at a small, [afternoon daily paper](http://timesrecord.com) in Brunswick. I developed a fondness for data reporting at [Mainebiz](http://mainebiz.com) before three years covering Maine's economy and investigations at the [Bangor Daily News](http://bangordailynews.com).

Witnessing some live data science and statistics wizardry one election night at the Bangor Daily News (when the paper had a data scientist on staff!) fascinated me and pushed me in a new direction. I received a master's degree in data science in the Fall 2024 from Northeastern University, through its new Portland, Maine, campus.
